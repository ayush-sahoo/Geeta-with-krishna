Refunds

Refund Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

We want Gita Verse to be worth it for you. This policy explains when you can get your money back for Annual Access (₹1,000 for 12 months).

7-day refund

If you are not satisfied, you can request a full refund within 7 days of your purchase. Email ayush@edumorph.in from the email address on your account with your payment ID or the date of purchase. After 7 days, purchases are non-refundable.

Duplicate or failed payments

If you were charged twice, or money was deducted but your access was not activated, contact us at any time and we will fix your access or refund the extra charge in full.

How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds are made to your original payment method through Razorpay.
  • We process approved refunds within 2 business days. Your bank or UPI app may then take 5–7 business days to show the credit.
  • When a refund is issued, Annual Access on that account ends.

No automatic renewals

Annual Access is a one-time payment and never renews automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. You will not be charged again unless you choose to buy another year.

Contact

For any refund or payment question, email ayush@edumorph.in. We usually reply within 2 business days.
