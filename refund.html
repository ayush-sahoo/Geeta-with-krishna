Refund Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy applies to Annual Access (₹1,000 for 12 months) and 3-Month Access (₹399 for 3 months) on Gita Verse.

No refunds

All purchases of Annual Access and 3-Month Access are final and non-refundable. Once your payment is successful and access is activated, we do not offer refunds or partial refunds, including for unused time.

You can explore today's verse and the free parts of Gita Verse before you buy.

Billing errors

If you were charged more than once for the same purchase, or money was deducted but your access was not activated, email support@edumorph.in with your payment ID or the date and time of payment. We will activate your access or return the extra charge to your original payment method through Razorpay.

No automatic renewals

Both plans are one-time payments and never renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. You will not be charged again unless you choose to buy again.

Contact

For any payment question, email support@edumorph.in. We usually reply within 2 business days.
