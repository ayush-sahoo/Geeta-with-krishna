Delete your account

Last updated: 19 October 2026

You can ask us to delete your Gita Verse account (on the website or in the Android app) and the data linked to it at any time.

Email us to delete your accountsupport@edumorph.in

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@edumorph.in with the subject "Delete my Gita Verse account".
  2. Write from the email address you sign in with, or include the mobile number you sign in with, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days and email you when it is done.

What we delete

  • Your account and sign-in details (email address or mobile number).
  • Your saved conversations with Krishna (typed and spoken).
  • Your usage records linked to your account.

What we keep

Records of payments (amount, date and payment ID) are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires, and then deleted. They are not used for anything else.

Delete only your conversations

To delete just your conversations with Krishna and keep your account, open Account and tap Delete my chat history. This takes effect immediately.
